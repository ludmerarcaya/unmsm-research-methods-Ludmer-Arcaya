# 🔴 RED — Never, Even if Nobody Would Know

I will never use Artificial Intelligence to:

- Write a thesis chapter and present it as my own original work.
- Fabricate experimental data.
- Manipulate results to improve model performance.
- Invent references or cite papers that I have not verified.
- Generate fake figures, tables, or experimental results.
- Modify statistical analyses to obtain desired conclusions.
- Hide the use of AI when disclosure is required.
- Allow AI to make scientific conclusions on my behalf.
- Submit AI-generated peer reviews.
- Present AI-generated ideas as original intellectual contributions without proper attribution.
- Circumvent university or journal policies regarding the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence.

The integrity of my research will always take precedence over productivity.
