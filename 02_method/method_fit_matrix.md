# Method Fit Matrix

## Research Question

**How can Bayesian Deep Learning and Generative AI simulate predictive uncertainty in citizen insecurity scenarios to support decision-making in Lima Metropolitana?**

---

# Candidate Methods Evaluated

The Method-Fit Matrix was applied to compare three candidate methodological approaches:

1. Design Science Research (DSR) with Quantitative AI Modeling
2. Pure Quantitative Predictive Modeling
3. Qualitative Case Study

The evaluation follows the E.D.F.C.V. framework presented in class:

* E = Epistemological Fit
* D = Data Availability
* F = Feasibility
* C = Contribution Type
* V = Venue Fit

Scores range from 1 (poor fit) to 5 (excellent fit).

# Criterion Justification

## E – Epistemological Fit

### Design Science Research + AI Modeling (5/5)

The research paradigm selected for this study combines Design Science Research and positivism. The primary objective is to build and evaluate an Artificial Intelligence artifact capable of predicting and simulating citizen insecurity scenarios. Therefore, DSR is fully aligned with the intended knowledge contribution.

### Pure Quantitative Modeling (4/5)

Quantitative modeling supports prediction and statistical validation but does not explicitly emphasize artifact construction, which is a central element of this research.

### Qualitative Case Study (2/5)

A qualitative case study would focus on perceptions and experiences rather than predictive modeling and simulation, making it poorly aligned with the research objectives.

---

## D – Data Availability

### Design Science Research + AI Modeling (4/5)

The study can leverage crime statistics, demographic indicators, geospatial information, and public security datasets available from government and open-data sources. However, data quality and completeness remain challenges.

### Pure Quantitative Modeling (4/5)

The same datasets are available for predictive analysis.

### Qualitative Case Study (3/5)

Interviews and institutional access may be difficult to obtain from public security organizations.

---

## F – Feasibility

### Design Science Research + AI Modeling (4/5)

The proposed approach is feasible within the doctoral timeframe because it relies on existing AI techniques, computational experimentation, and publicly available datasets. Additional effort is required for simulation design and uncertainty modeling.

### Pure Quantitative Modeling (5/5)

This approach is technically simpler and easier to implement.

### Qualitative Case Study (4/5)

Feasible from a methodological perspective but dependent on stakeholder participation and institutional approvals.

---

## C – Contribution Type

### Design Science Research + AI Modeling (5/5)

The expected contribution is the creation of a novel Bayesian Artificial Intelligence framework integrating Bayesian Deep Learning and Generative AI for predictive uncertainty simulation. This represents a significant artifact-oriented contribution.

### Pure Quantitative Modeling (3/5)

Would mainly provide predictive performance results without producing a new decision-support artifact.

### Qualitative Case Study (2/5)

Would contribute contextual insights but not a computational framework.

---

## V – Venue Fit

### Design Science Research + AI Modeling (5/5)

The proposed methodology aligns well with leading Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Smart Cities, and Decision Support Systems journals and conferences.

### Pure Quantitative Modeling (4/5)

Suitable for predictive analytics venues but offers less methodological novelty.

### Qualitative Case Study (2/5)

Less aligned with the target publication venues associated with Artificial Intelligence and computational research.

---

# Selected Method

Based on the Method-Fit Matrix evaluation, **Design Science Research combined with Bayesian Deep Learning and Generative AI** achieved the highest score (23/25).

This method provides the strongest alignment with the research paradigm, available data, expected scientific contribution, and target publication venues. It also supports the development and evaluation of an Artificial Intelligence artifact capable of predicting and simulating citizen insecurity scenarios under uncertainty in Lima Metropolitana.

## Defended Winner

**Design Science Research (DSR) + Bayesian Deep Learning + Generative AI**

**Final Score: 23/25**
