# Mini Systematic Literature Review

## Title

Bayesian Deep Learning and Generative AI for Predictive Uncertainty Simulation in Citizen Insecurity Scenarios: A Mini Systematic Review

---

## Research Question

How can Bayesian Deep Learning and Generative AI simulate predictive uncertainty in citizen insecurity scenarios to support decision-making in Lima Metropolitana?

---

## Review Objective

The objective of this review is to identify, analyze, and synthesize recent scientific evidence regarding the use of Bayesian Artificial Intelligence, Bayesian Deep Learning, Generative AI, and predictive analytics for crime prediction, public security management, uncertainty quantification, and urban risk simulation.

## Synthesis of Findings

Three major themes emerged from the literature.

### Theme 1: Bayesian Deep Learning for Uncertainty Estimation

Several studies demonstrated that Bayesian Deep Learning models provide more reliable predictions than deterministic machine learning approaches because they explicitly quantify uncertainty. This capability is particularly valuable in high-stakes domains where incorrect predictions may lead to significant consequences.

### Theme 2: Generative AI for Scenario Simulation

Generative AI techniques have shown promising results for generating synthetic scenarios and supporting strategic planning. However, their application to public security and crime prediction remains limited.

### Theme 3: AI-Based Decision Support Systems

The literature consistently highlights the importance of integrating predictive models into decision-support frameworks rather than treating prediction as the final objective. Effective systems combine prediction, uncertainty estimation, explainability, and simulation.

---

## Preliminary Conclusions

The literature confirms the potential of Bayesian Artificial Intelligence for uncertainty-aware prediction and decision support. Generative AI provides complementary capabilities through scenario generation and simulation.

However, there is limited evidence regarding the integration of Bayesian Deep Learning and Generative AI within a unified framework for citizen insecurity management, particularly in Latin America.

This observation motivates the proposed doctoral research.
