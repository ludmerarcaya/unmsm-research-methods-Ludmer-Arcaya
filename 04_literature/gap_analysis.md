## Knowledge Gap

The literature contains numerous studies on Bayesian Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI independently. However, very few studies investigate the integration of both approaches within a unified framework for citizen insecurity management.

### Evidence

Studies on Bayesian Deep Learning focus primarily on uncertainty estimation, while Generative AI studies emphasize synthetic data generation and scenario simulation. The combination of both technologies remains largely unexplored.

---

## Methodological Gap

Most crime prediction studies employ traditional machine learning techniques such as Random Forests, Gradient Boosting, Support Vector Machines, and Deep Neural Networks.

### Evidence

Only a limited number of studies apply Bayesian Deep Learning approaches capable of quantifying predictive uncertainty.

### Opportunity

The proposed research introduces Bayesian Deep Learning as a methodological innovation for public security analytics.

---

## Contextual Gap

The majority of AI-based public security research has been conducted in developed countries.

### Evidence

Most datasets originate from the United States, China, the United Kingdom, and European Union countries.

### Opportunity

There is limited evidence regarding the application of Bayesian AI in Peru and Latin America, particularly in Lima Metropolitana.

---

## Theoretical Gap

Current predictive frameworks focus on maximizing prediction accuracy but often ignore uncertainty communication and scenario exploration.

### Evidence

Few studies provide a comprehensive framework connecting prediction, uncertainty quantification, simulation, and decision support.

### Opportunity

This research seeks to establish a theoretical and practical framework linking Bayesian uncertainty estimation with Generative AI simulation for public security decision-making.

---

## Final Research Gap

The literature reveals the absence of an integrated Bayesian Artificial Intelligence framework that combines Bayesian Deep Learning and Generative AI to predict, quantify uncertainty, and simulate citizen insecurity scenarios in Lima Metropolitana.

This gap represents the primary scientific contribution targeted by the proposed doctoral research.
